1️⃣ Visual Flowchart: The 4 Signs of a Clear Noun (عَلَامَاتُ الإِسْمِ الظَّاهِرِ — Pages 8–10)

SIGN 1: Tanween (ـٌ / ـٍ / ـً)
بَيْتٌ 🔊
Singular indefinite noun ("A house").
SIGN 2: Alif & Laam (الـ)
البَيْتُ 🔊
Definite ("The house") — drops Tanween.
SIGN 3: Possessive Pronoun
بَيْتِي / بَيْتُكَ 🔊
Attached pronoun at end: ـي (My) / ـكَ (Your).
SIGN 4: Jar Particle (حرف جر)
مِنْ بَيْتٍ • مِنَ البَيْتِ 🔊
Preposition before noun forces Kasrah (ـِ / ـٍ).
